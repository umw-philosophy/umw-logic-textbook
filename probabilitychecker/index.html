Worked solution

    Reference

    The basic rules
    • Equally likely outcomes: P(A) = favorable outcomes ÷ total outcomes.
    • Complement rule: P(not A) = 1 − P(A).
    • Addition rule (mutually exclusive): P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B).
    • General addition rule: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B).
    Conditional probability
    • P(A | B) = P(A and B) ÷ P(B).
    • Multiplication rule: P(A and B) = P(A) · P(B | A).
    • Independent events: P(A and B) = P(A) · P(B).
    • “At least one” is the complement of “none.”
    Bayes' theorem
    • P(H | E) = P(E | H) · P(H) ÷ [ P(E | H) · P(H) + P(E | ~H) · P(~H) ].
    • P(H) is the prior, or base rate. P(~H) = 1 − P(H).
    • P(E | H) is how often the evidence appears when H is true.
    • P(E | ~H) is how often the evidence appears when H is false.
    A standard deck of cards
    • 52 cards in four suits of 13: hearts and diamonds (red), clubs and spades (black).
    • Each suit has an ace, the numbers 2 through 10, and three face cards: jack, queen, king.
    • So there are 12 face cards and 4 cards of each rank.
